Privacy

Playing the games needs no account. You only need one to share your own game. This page says what is stored if you make one.

What is stored

  • Your email address, kept by Supabase, which handles sign-in.
  • Your handle and display name.
  • The details of games you submit: name, description, link and thumbnail.
  • Your sign-in session, kept in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Where it lives

Account and game details are stored in Supabase. Uploaded thumbnail images are stored in Vercel Blob. The site itself is hosted on Vercel.

Feedback

Anyone can send feedback without an account. The feedback form stores your message, the contact you choose to give (optional), your browser's user-agent string and a hashed version of your IP address, in a private Vercel Blob. I can remove a message on request (contact below).

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no tracking cookies. Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless.

Delete your data

Sign in, open your account page and press Delete account. This removes your account, your profile and all your games. Your games leave the live site after the next site rebuild, which deleting your account starts. Uploaded thumbnail image files are not removed by that button, and they stay viewable by their link until I remove them, so message me if you want them gone too.

Contact
