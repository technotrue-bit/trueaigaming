Privacy

Playing the games needs no account. You only need one to share your own game or to keep your progress in some games on every device. This page says what is stored if you make one, and what the site measures when you visit.

What is stored

  • Your email address, kept by Supabase, which handles sign-in.
  • Your handle and display name.
  • The details of games you submit: name, description, link and thumbnail.
  • Your sign-in session, kept in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • Your saved progress in games that offer account saves (for now, Flag Catch: points, unlocks, best scores, matches played and won), so it follows you when you sign in on another device. The save also holds a random id for each device you play on, so progress from two devices adds up. It is only stored while you are signed in on the play page.

Without an account, every game keeps its progress on your device only. Aurora's World, our game for young children, never uses accounts: its progress stays on the device, and its "Move to a new phone" code goes straight from one screen to the other without passing through this site's servers.

Age

Accounts are for people 13 and up. The sign-up form asks for your birth month and year to check this. The date itself is not kept or sent anywhere: the account only records that the check was passed. If you are under 13, you can still play every game without an account. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, message me (contact below) and I will delete it.

Bot check

The sign-up, sign-in and password reset forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to stop automated sign-ups. Turnstile runs in your browser on the account page only, and Cloudflare sees technical details such as your IP address and browser to tell people from bots. Its own privacy policy covers what it does with them.

Where it lives

Account and game details are stored in Supabase. Uploaded thumbnail images are stored in Vercel Blob. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Account emails, such as the email check and password reset, are sent through an email delivery service on the site's behalf, which sees your address only to deliver them.

Feedback

Anyone can send feedback without an account. The feedback form stores your message, the contact you choose to give (optional), your browser's user-agent string and a hashed version of your IP address, in a private Vercel Blob. I can remove a message on request (contact below).

Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to see which games people play and how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) so it can tell visits apart. It records the pages you view, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), your device and browser, and these events: which game card you click, when a game starts on the play page and how long it was played, when feedback is sent (only the game and whether it was a bug or a suggestion, never your message or contact) and when you copy text on a game page. Google handles this data under its privacy policy. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings, a content blocker or Google's opt-out add-on.

Page views and the same events are also counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

Delete your data

Sign in, open your account page and press Delete account. This removes your account, your profile, your saved game progress and all your games. Your games leave the live site after the next site rebuild, which deleting your account starts. Uploaded thumbnail image files are not removed by that button, and they stay viewable by their link until I remove them, so message me if you want them gone too.
